Starless Night — working draft
Can I steal your silence / Can I still your fears / Set fire to your loneliness / Drain your pool of tears / Banish all your demons / Watch them — waiting for your star to rise Can I steal your silence / On this starless starless night Search the skies for signs of life / On this starless starless night / There's a silent silent space / Beneath the screaming of the world / Where you sing your song now Let's magic darkness into light / On this starless starless night
The repetition is the point — starless starless, silent silent. The second word doesn't intensify, it settles. The whole song is doing that: not more of the feeling but deeper into it.
